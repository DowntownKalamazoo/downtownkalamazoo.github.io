Farmers Alley is one of the first two placemaking sites to move to construction. The alley becomes a pocket park: a small, comfortable place to sit, meet up or pass through on the way across downtown.

## What's changing

- **New paving** across the plaza
- **Lighting**, so the alley feels welcoming after dark
- **Public art**
- **Landscaping**, with places to sit
- **A stronger link to Bates Alley**, so the two alleys work together as one walking route

## Why here

Many people already cut through downtown's alleys to avoid busy one-way streets. The placemaking plan makes those routes good places to spend time, not just shortcuts.
