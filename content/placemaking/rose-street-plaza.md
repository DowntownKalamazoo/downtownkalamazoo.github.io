The Rose Street plaza sits where downtown meets Arcadia Creek. It's one of the first two placemaking sites to move to construction.

## What's changing

- **A redesigned plaza**, with new materials and landscaping
- **Lighting** for evenings and events
- **A better connection to the Arcadia Creek trail**, so it's easier to walk between the plaza, the creek and the festival grounds

## Nearby

Arcadia Creek Festival Place, next door, is being renovated as a separate project. See [Projects](../projects.html) for its schedule.
