South Burdick, from Lovell Street to Cedar Street, is planned as a festival street. It's designed so the block can close to traffic several times a year for festivals, markets and pop-up events, then open again.

## What's planned

- **Room for events**, with a street that's easy to close and set up
- **A design that matches the North Kalamazoo Mall**, so the whole length of Burdick feels connected
- **Better for walking and biking** every day, not just on event days

The schedule depends on when the rest of the funding is secured.
