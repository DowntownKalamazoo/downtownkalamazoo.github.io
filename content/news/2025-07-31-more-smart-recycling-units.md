The City of Kalamazoo announced it would remove 58 aging trash cans downtown and replace them with **26 new Bigbelly units**, bringing the total to **51 dual trash and recycling bins**. Some existing units were moved to busier spots.

Each Bigbelly unit has two sides, one for trash and one for recycling. Sensors let the Downtown Ambassadors know when a unit is full, so they can empty it before it overflows. The goal is less overflow, more reliable pickup times and a cleaner downtown.

The expansion is funded by the **Downtown Economic Growth Authority (DEGA)**.

## Recycling right

Interns from Western Michigan University's Industrial-Organizational Behavior Management program audited the downtown units and found that **nearly 44% of what went into the recycling side was contamination**, like food waste or items that can't be recycled. The results are guiding new signs and instructions on the units.
