*Update: this year's application window closed on March 27, 2026. The Michigan Economic Development Corporation expected to announce awards in July 2026.*

The City of Kalamazoo invited downtown businesses to apply for **Match on Main**, a competitive grant program from the Michigan Economic Development Corporation (MEDC) that supports place-based businesses.

Local governments and downtown organizations apply on behalf of the businesses they select. The City could apply for up to **$25,000 in reimbursement funding** for two eligible downtown businesses, and each business provides a **10% cash match**.

## Who could apply

- Businesses in a traditional downtown, historic neighborhood commercial corridor or concentrated commercial area in Kalamazoo
- With a physical storefront and face-to-face sales
- That have, or can get, site control before applying
- Michigan-based for-profit or nonprofit businesses

Franchises, strip-mall locations, big-box retailers, and businesses that mainly sell marijuana, CBD or tobacco weren't eligible. Most businesses that already received Match on Main funding weren't either.

## Key dates

- **March 27, 2026, 5 p.m.:** completed worksheets due
- **April 10, 2026:** selected businesses notified
- **July 2026:** MEDC award announcements expected
