Shoppers, diners and visitors in downtown Kalamazoo can now recycle their take-out cups and containers on the go. The City of Kalamazoo and Downtown Kalamazoo have launched a new campaign encouraging people to recycle clean, empty paper cups, plastic cups and take-out containers in recycling bins throughout downtown.

These items are already accepted in residential recycling carts across the city. Now more than 87,000 Kalamazoo residents and the many visitors who come downtown each year can recycle where they live, work, shop and dine.

“We want recycling to be just as easy downtown as it is at the curb,” said Meghan Behymer, downtown coordinator for the City of Kalamazoo. “Adding take-out cups and containers to our downtown recycling bins makes it easier for residents and visitors to recycle right and helps keep these materials out of the landfill.”

The campaign kicked off November 21 at the Kalamazoo Holiday Parade presented by Fox Motors, where residents and downtown visitors saw updated signs on downtown recycling bins and received an insert in the holiday shopping bags handed out along the route. The insert explains how to recycle take-out cups and containers, with a focus on the newest item, paper cups.

## How to recycle your cup

Items should be clean and empty before recycling. For paper and plastic cups, that means pouring out any leftover liquid, tossing straws in the trash, and recycling the lid, sleeve and cup loose in the recycling bin. The campaign also includes a recycling video, digital ads and social media that encourage residents to follow these steps at home and downtown.

## About the partnership

The campaign is part of the Foodservice Packaging Institute’s (FPI) community partnership program, supported by a communications grant from FPI with co-funding from the NextGen Consortium. FPI is the trade association for the foodservice packaging industry in North America, working to expand recycling access for foodservice packaging such as cups, containers, boxes and paper bags. The NextGen Consortium is an industry collaboration managed by Closed Loop Partners’ Center for the Circular Economy, working to increase the circularity of foodservice packaging, with a focus on cups.

“Paper cups and other take-out containers are valuable materials, and access to recycling them is growing,” said Natha Dempsey, president of the Foodservice Packaging Institute. “We’re proud to support Kalamazoo in making these foodservice packaging items as easy to recycle downtown as they are at home, so they can be made into new products.”

## Made into new packaging, here in Kalamazoo

Paper cups recycled in Kalamazoo can come back as new packaging close to home. Graphic Packaging International (GPI), an FPI member that operates a paper mill in Kalamazoo, buys recovered paper cups and turns that fiber into new paperboard packaging, including cereal and food boxes, beverage carriers and even new paper cups. GPI employees joined the campaign launch at the holiday parade and will appear at other events throughout the season, talking with residents and downtown visitors about how the cups they recycle are used at the Kalamazoo mill to make new packaging.
