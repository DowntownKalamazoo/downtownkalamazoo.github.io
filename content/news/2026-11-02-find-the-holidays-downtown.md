**KALAMAZOO, Mich. —** Downtown Kalamazoo today launched Find the Holidays Downtown, a campaign running through Dec. 31, along with a new year-round website for downtown, [downtownkalamazoo.us](../index.html). The site’s holiday section puts the season’s events, a new online gift guide and 50 ready-made day plans in one place at [downtownkalamazoo.us/holidays](../holidays/index.html).

The campaign is built around curiosity: an invitation to slow down, wander the Kalamazoo Mall and the streets around it, and find the shops, restaurants and traditions that make downtown Kalamazoo unlike anywhere else.

“Downtown rewards the curious,” said Meghan Behymer, Downtown Coordinator for Downtown Kalamazoo. “Turn any corner this season and you’ll find something you didn’t know you were looking for, whether it’s the perfect gift, a new favorite spot for hot cocoa or the story behind a building you’ve walked past a hundred times. And every gift you find here supports a neighbor’s business.”

## Three ways to plan a downtown holiday

**[Holiday Gift Guide](../holidays/gifts.html).** The new guide features 76 gifts from 41 downtown businesses, each linked to the shop that sells it. Shoppers can filter the list or tap “Surprise me.”

**[Plan Your Day](../holidays/plan.html).** Fifty ready-made itineraries, from a family afternoon to a Friday night out, can be sorted by who’s coming, which day and budget. Each lists its stops, that day’s Santa’s Workshop and Holly Jolly Trolley hours, and where to park. Visitors can also build their own day from the [downtown business directory](../directory.html).

**[Downtown Dollars](../dollars.html).** Downtown Kalamazoo’s gift certificates are accepted at 41 participating downtown businesses. Since the program began in 2019, shoppers have spent more than $390,000 in Downtown Dollars downtown.

## A new home for downtown online

The holiday section is part of the new downtownkalamazoo.us, which brings downtown together in one place year-round. A [business directory](../directory.html) lists 170 downtown businesses with their hours, and shows which ones accept Downtown Dollars and which are in the [Social District](../social-district.html). The [events calendar](../events.html) can be viewed by month or as a list, and groups and venues can [submit their own events](../submit-event.html). A [Visit & Park](../visit.html) page explains where to park, what it costs and how to get around. Visitors can also [sign up for the downtown newsletter](../newsletter.html).

## $2,500 in surprises on Small Business Saturday

From 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. on Saturday, Nov. 28, Downtown Kalamazoo staff and Downtown Ambassadors will hand out 250 envelopes of Downtown Dollars across downtown, each holding $5 to $20. Envelopes are free, one per person, with no purchase or sign-up required, while supplies last. Staff will be at the corner of the Kalamazoo Mall and Michigan Avenue throughout the giveaway. Santa’s Workshop opens at noon the same day. [See the Small Business Saturday details](../holidays/sbs.html).

## Holiday calendar highlights

- **Nov. 6, from 5 p.m.:** [First Friday & Art Hop](../holidays/events/first-friday-nov.html), galleries, shops and studios downtown (hosted by the Arts Council of Greater Kalamazoo)
- **Nov. 21, 11 a.m.:** [Kalamazoo Holiday Parade presented by Fox Motors](../holidays/events/holiday-parade.html), followed by [After Parade Activities](../holidays/events/after-parade.html) on the Kalamazoo Mall, noon to 3 p.m.
- **Nov. 27, 5 to 7 p.m.:** [Tree Lighting Ceremony](../holidays/events/tree-lighting.html), Bronson Park; lights on at about 6 p.m. (hosted by the City of Kalamazoo)
- **Nov. 27 to Dec. 31:** [Holly Jolly Trolley](../holidays/events/holly-jolly-trolley.html), free rides from City Hall, Thursdays through Sundays plus Dec. 21–23 and New Year’s Eve (run by KELC Events, with sponsors)
- **Nov. 28, 11 a.m. to 1 p.m.:** [Small Business Saturday Downtown Dollars giveaway](../holidays/sbs.html) (hosted by Downtown Kalamazoo)
- **Nov. 28 to Dec. 24:** [Santa’s Workshop](../holidays/events/santas-workshop.html), free, City Centre, 125 S. Kalamazoo Mall, Thursdays through Sundays plus Dec. 21–23 (hosted by the Kalamazoo Experiential Learning Center)
- **Dec. 4 to 19:** [Gingerbread House Contest](../holidays/events/gingerbread-wonderland.html), downtown shop windows (hosted by Community Homeworks)
- **Dec. 10, 5:30 to 6:30 p.m.:** [December to Remember](../holidays/events/december-to-remember.html), Bronson Park (hosted by the Kalamazoo Youth Action Council and Kzoo Parks)
- **Dec. 11, 6 to 11 p.m.:** [Moonlight Madness](../holidays/events/moonlight-madness.html), late hours at shops, restaurants and bars (hosted by the Downtown Kalamazoo Collective)
- **Dec. 31, 6 p.m. to midnight:** [New Year’s Fest](../holidays/events/new-years-fest.html), Bronson Park and downtown venues

Hours, parking and full event details are at [downtownkalamazoo.us/holidays](../holidays/index.html).

## About Downtown Kalamazoo

Downtown Kalamazoo is served by the Downtown Development Authority (DDA) and the Downtown Economic Growth Authority (DEGA), two public authorities that share a board and work in partnership with the City of Kalamazoo. Downtown staff and the Downtown Ambassadors support the businesses, events and public spaces that make downtown a place to live, work and visit. Learn more at [downtownkalamazoo.us](../index.html).
