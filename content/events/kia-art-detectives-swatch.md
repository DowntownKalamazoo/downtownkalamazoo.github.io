It might be gray outside, but the KIA is bursting with color. Art Detectives reads about Swatch, a girl who loves colors and wants to collect them all. Then families search the KIA’s collection for colors and make a colorful masterpiece to take home.

**Cost:** Museum admission: $10 for adults and $5 for children, with discounts for some visitors.

**Who it’s for:** Families and kids.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
