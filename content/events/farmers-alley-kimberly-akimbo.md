Kimberly is about to turn 16, but a rare genetic condition makes her look like a woman in her 70s. New in suburban New Jersey, she's juggling a chaotic family, a first crush and a scheme that could land everyone in trouble, and she keeps her sense of humor through all of it. The musical has music by Jeanine Tesori and a book and lyrics by David Lindsay-Abaire, and it won the Tony Award for Best Musical.
