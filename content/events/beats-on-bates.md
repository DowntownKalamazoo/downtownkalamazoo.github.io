Take a midweek break downtown. Every Wednesday from June through September, Bates Alley fills with live music, with a different lineup of performers each week. Meet friends for dinner, bring a chair or blanket, and stay for the evening.

Food and drinks are available at the restaurants and bars around the alley. Bates Alley is part of the downtown [Social District](../social-district.html), so you can carry a drink from a participating business in an approved Social District cup.

Beats on Bates is free. This season's performers will be posted closer to summer.
