Know every chorus but can’t remember where you put your keys? Music bingo swaps the numbers for familiar songs. Listen for each one, mark it on your card, and see if you can be the first to call out “Bingo!”

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** Young adults and adults 21+.
