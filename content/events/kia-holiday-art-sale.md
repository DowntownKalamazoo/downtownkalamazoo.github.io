Find a one-of-a-kind gift at this three-day holiday sale, which supports both area artists and programs at the Kirk Newman Art School (KNAS).

- **Thursday, December 3, 4–8 p.m.:** Members’ Night, for KIA members
- **Friday, December 4, 4–8 p.m.:** Open to the public
- **Saturday, December 5, 9 a.m.–3 p.m.:** Open to the public

Friday is also December’s Art Hop, so it’s an easy night to make the sale part of a downtown gallery walk.

**Cost:** Free to attend.

**Who it’s for:** Everyone.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
