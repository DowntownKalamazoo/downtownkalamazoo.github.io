Bring a puzzle (or a few) you’re ready to pass on, browse what others brought, and go home with your next challenge. Meet other puzzle fans over a glass of wine.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

[Sit and Stay Winery](../directory.html#b-sit-and-stay-winery) is a dog-friendly winery at 260 E. Michigan Ave. that takes Downtown Dollars.
