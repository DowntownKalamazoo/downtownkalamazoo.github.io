After a chance encounter at the Metropolitan Museum of Art, Daphne Fuller rekindles a tender relationship with her estranged stepfather, Eddie Trott. Years have passed, but their early bond pulls them back together. Woven through their story is a horse named Whistler, and both learn that family comes in many forms.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
