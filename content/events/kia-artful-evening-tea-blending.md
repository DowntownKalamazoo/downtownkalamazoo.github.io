Learn the art of tea blending with Kama Tai. Craft your own blends by hand while learning about the many meanings tea has held across cultures and centuries. While you’re there, see *Chanoyu: Modern Tea Ceramics from the Horvitz Collection* in the Joy Light Gallery.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
