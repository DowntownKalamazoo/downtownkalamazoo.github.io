Kelly Peck, co-curator of *Statement Pieces: Fashioning the Self & Society* and Vice President of Barrett Barrera Projects, joins Rehema Barber, the KIA’s Director of Curatorial Affairs, for a conversation about the exhibition and its presentation at the KIA. It’s a preview the night before the opening reception.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
