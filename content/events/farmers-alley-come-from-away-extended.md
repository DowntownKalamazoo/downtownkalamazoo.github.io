*Come From Away* has been extended through December 6, with five added performances:

- **Thursday, December 3:** 7:30 p.m.
- **Friday, December 4:** 7:30 p.m.
- **Saturday, December 5:** 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m.
- **Sunday, December 6:** 2 p.m.

When 38 planes were diverted to Gander, Newfoundland, on September 11, 2001, the town opened its homes, schools and churches to thousands of strangers. The musical tells that true story with a folk-rock score and a cast that plays both the locals and the passengers. This is a return engagement of the fastest-selling show in Farmers Alley Theatre’s history.

Tickets are available from Farmers Alley Theatre online or by calling the box office at (269) 343-2727.
