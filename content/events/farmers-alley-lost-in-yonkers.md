In 1942, two teenage brothers are left with their formidable grandmother in Yonkers, New York, while their father travels for work. There they get to know their aunt Bella, who has a secret romance, and their uncle Louie, a small-time hoodlum. Neil Simon's memory play won the Pulitzer Prize for Drama. D. Terry Williams directs.

Farmers Alley had to cancel this production when it was set to open in March 2020, so this staging is a long time coming.
