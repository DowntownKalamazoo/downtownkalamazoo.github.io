Educators of every grade level and subject are invited for a free evening of creativity and connection. Explore the museum’s resources for students, try gallery activities, make some art and enjoy food and drinks with colleagues. The Gallery Shop is open, and raffle prizes are drawn throughout the evening.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** Teachers and educators of all grade levels and subjects.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
