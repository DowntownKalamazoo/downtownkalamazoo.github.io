Jenna is a waitress and gifted pie maker stuck in an unhappy marriage in a small town. When a baking contest in the next county offers a way out, she starts to imagine a different life, with help from her coworkers and an unexpected new friendship. The musical is based on the film, with music and lyrics by Sara Bareilles and a book by Jessie Nelson.

**ASL interpreted performance:** Saturday, July 17, at 7:30 p.m.

Tickets go on sale March 18, 2027, from Farmers Alley Theatre online or by calling the box office at (269) 343-2727.
