Take a Wonder Walk through the galleries, looking closely at a few animal artworks and playing gallery games along the way. It’s a gentle way to introduce little ones to art and the museum. Wonder Walks are planned for children 2 to 4, but everyone is welcome. Caregivers stay with their children the whole time, and registering ahead is encouraged.

**Cost:** Free with museum admission ($5 to $10).

**Who it’s for:** Families with young children, ages 2 to 4.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the first floor. The KIA has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.

[Kalamazoo Institute of Arts](../directory.html#b-kalamazoo-institute-of-arts) is downtown’s art museum, at 314 S. Park St. near Bronson Park.
