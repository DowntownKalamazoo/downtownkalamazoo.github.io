*Statement Pieces: Fashioning the Self & Society* opens to the public on November 7 and stays on view through February 7, 2027. The exhibition sets works from the KIA’s collection beside contemporary fashion from the Barrett Barrera Projects collection to look at how self-presentation and identity have been shaped and reimagined over the last 200 years.

**Cost:** Museum admission, $5 to $10.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the first floor. The KIA has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.

[Kalamazoo Institute of Arts](../directory.html#b-kalamazoo-institute-of-arts) is downtown’s art museum, at 314 S. Park St. near Bronson Park.
