In this ArtBreak, Iris Carney Cooper talks about the ideas from art, history and fashion that let designers shape the human form to tell a story. What goes into a garment’s construction to tell a larger story? This look at the craft shows how to spot clues in clothing by exploring fashion trends, symbolism and the expectations of society.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the first floor. The KIA has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.

[Kalamazoo Institute of Arts](../directory.html#b-kalamazoo-institute-of-arts) is downtown’s art museum, at 314 S. Park St. near Bronson Park.
