Books, art and animals come together. Give discarded books a new life by folding their pages into animals with a series of simple folds.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** Families, kids, students and young adults.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
