In this lecture, Dr. Meghen Jones looks at how Japan’s leading ceramists carry forward the Way of Tea through their materials, processes and forms. It’s a good introduction to *Chanoyu: Modern Tea Ceramics from the Horvitz Collection*, which opens at the KIA two days later.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
