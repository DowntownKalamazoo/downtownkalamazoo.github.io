Pick a template and create your own clothing silhouette, then style it with patterns and contrasts cut from collage paper and book pages. Look to the works in *Statement Pieces* for inspiration.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** Families and kids.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
