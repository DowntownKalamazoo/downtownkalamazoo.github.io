Be among the first to see *Statement Pieces: Fashioning the Self & Society*, which looks at how fashion and the visual arts shape the way we understand and present who we are. Brief remarks start at 5:30 p.m. After that, explore the galleries and browse exhibition-related items in the Gallery Shop.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the first floor. The KIA has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.

[Kalamazoo Institute of Arts](../directory.html#b-kalamazoo-institute-of-arts) is downtown’s art museum, at 314 S. Park St. near Bronson Park.
