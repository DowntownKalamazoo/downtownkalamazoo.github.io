How do fashion and fine art come together to tell a story? How can choices of color, shape and texture comment on personal, social and cultural identities and values? Join the conversation about selected fashion pieces and artworks in *Statement Pieces: Fashioning the Self & Society*.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
