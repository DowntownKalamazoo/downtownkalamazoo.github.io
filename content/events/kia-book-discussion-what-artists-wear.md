Covering a wide range of artists and styles, from Basquiat to Warhol, Charlie Porter builds a sweeping story of how artists use clothing: to shape a personal identity, to extend their art or to influence how the public sees them. The book’s essays, reflections and interviews offer a fresh way to understand art in its many forms.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
