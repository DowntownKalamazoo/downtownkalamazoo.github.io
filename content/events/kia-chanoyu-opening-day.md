*Chanoyu: Modern Tea Ceramics from the Horvitz Collection* opens November 21. Drawn from the collection of Carol and Jeffrey Horvitz, it brings together Japanese tea bowls (chawan) and water jars (mizusashi) that carry the quiet power and refined sensibility of the tea ceremony and Japanese aesthetics.

**Cost:** Museum admission, $5 to $10.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
