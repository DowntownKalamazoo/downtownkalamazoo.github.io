College students from WMU, Kalamazoo College, KVCC and other schools get the museum to themselves for a night of games, food, music, raffles and interactive activities. Explore the galleries, make something and meet students from other campuses.

**Cost:** Free with a college ID.

**Who it’s for:** College students.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
