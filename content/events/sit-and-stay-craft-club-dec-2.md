Make a little time for creativity. Bring friends or come meet new ones, and spend a relaxed evening crafting and chatting with your favorite wine or a mocktail. Craft Club meets twice in December, on Wednesday, December 2, and Wednesday, December 16.

**Cost:** $10 to $40.

**Who it’s for:** All ages, including families and kids.
