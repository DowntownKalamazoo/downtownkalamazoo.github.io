Art Detectives starts with a book about a stylish vampire and his passion for fashion. Then families head into *Statement Pieces: Fashioning the Self & Society* to find outfits by famous designers alongside artworks from the KIA’s collection, and finish by making a fashion-inspired art project to take home.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** Families and kids.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
