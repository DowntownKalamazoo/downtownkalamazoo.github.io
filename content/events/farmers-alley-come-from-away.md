When 38 planes were diverted to Gander, Newfoundland, on September 11, 2001, the town opened its homes, schools and churches to thousands of strangers. *Come From Away* tells that true story with a folk-rock score and a cast that plays both the locals and the passengers.

This is a return engagement of the fastest-selling show in Farmers Alley Theatre's history, with much of the original creative team back and a new revolving stage.

**ASL interpreted performance:** Sunday, November 15, at 2 p.m.

Tickets are available from Farmers Alley Theatre online or by calling the box office at (269) 343-2727.
