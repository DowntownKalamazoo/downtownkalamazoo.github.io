Rupert Holmes, the Tony Award-winning writer of *The Mystery of Edwin Drood*, built *Accomplice* to keep the audience guessing. What starts as a murder plot in an English country cottage keeps flipping, until it's hard to tell the play from the plot. It won the Edgar Award from the Mystery Writers of America. Mark Liermann directs.

**ASL interpreted performance:** Sunday, February 7, at 2 p.m.

Tickets are available from Farmers Alley Theatre online or by calling the box office at (269) 343-2727.
