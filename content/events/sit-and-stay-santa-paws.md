Keep the holiday fun going after the [Kalamazoo Holiday Parade](../holidays/events/holiday-parade.html). Bring your furry best friend for a picture with Santa Paws, ready for this year’s holiday card.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages, including families and kids (and pets).

[Sit and Stay Winery](../directory.html#b-sit-and-stay-winery) is a dog-friendly winery at 260 E. Michigan Ave. that takes Downtown Dollars.
