This ArtBreak goes offsite to the KIA’s neighbor, the Kalamazoo Civic Theatre. *Statement Pieces* looks at fashion and self-expression; this program looks at how clothing establishes the vital parts of a character onstage.

**Cost:** Free.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.
