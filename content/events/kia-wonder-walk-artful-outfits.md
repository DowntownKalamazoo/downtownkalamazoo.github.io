Get ready for some fashion fun. The youngest art lovers can explore *Statement Pieces: Fashioning the Self & Society* and see how works from the KIA’s collection are paired with outfits by world-famous designers. This relaxed gallery visit is designed especially for young children and their caregivers.

**Cost:** Museum admission: $10 for adults and $5 for children, with discounts for some visitors.

**Who it’s for:** Young children and their caregivers.

**Accessibility:** This program is on the KIA’s first floor. The building has exterior ramps, automatic doors and an elevator.
