Vintage in the Zoo and Old Dog Tavern are teaming up for a holiday pop-up on Black Friday. Shop 13 local vintage and handmade vendors, enjoy food and cocktail specials, and dance to live vinyl from DJ Dan Steely. It’s a family-friendly, indoor way to shop small and support local businesses this season.

**Cost:** Free to attend.

**Who it’s for:** All ages.
